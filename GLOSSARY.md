# Glossary

- **Saved library**: Track and playlist metadata successfully stored on the device. It may contain no songs and does not imply that music files are downloaded.
- **Local library readiness**: The result of loading the saved library for browsing. It is independent of remote refresh progress or connectivity.
- **Downloaded track**: A track whose music file is present on the device and can be played without a network connection.
- **Offline playlist**: A playlist explicitly selected for preparation on the watch. Its downloaded music is retained until the selection is cancelled or removed; selecting playlist metadata on the phone does not select it for offline preparation.
- **Offline preparation**: Downloading every missing track in an offline playlist while the watch app is open, independently of playback. Ready means every selected track is present on disk.
- **Downloaded-only playback**: A queue built from tracks already on disk, with music streaming, playback downloads, and now-playing artwork fetches disabled for that queue.
